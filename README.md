# 🎬 IMDb Movie Database & Box Office Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI** business intelligence dashboard providing deep exploratory data analysis on the global film industry using the **IMDb Movie Database**. The project investigates box office gross revenues, production budgets, audience IMDb scores, and social media engagement metrics across actors and directors.

---

## 📌 Project Overview & Insights

- **Box Office Economics:** Correlation between production budget and global gross revenue, identifying the most profitable genres (Action, Adventure, Comedy).
- **Critical & Audience Reception:** Distribution of IMDb scores across content ratings (`PG`, `PG-13`, `R`, `G`) and release decades.
- **Talent & Star Power Impact:** Analysis of lead actor and director social engagement (Facebook likes) against movie commercial success.
- **Trend & Historical Analysis:** Longitudinal analysis of film releases, durations, and revenue trends from classic cinema to modern releases.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs & Metrics

- **Total Box Office Gross Revenue:** Aggregate revenue generated across cataloged titles.
- **Total Production Budget:** Total financial capital invested in productions.
- **Average IMDb Rating:** Central tendency of audience and critic scores.
- **Profitability & ROI:** Return on investment across cinematic categories and studios.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **ETL & Data Cleaning:** Power Query (missing value imputation, data type formatting, column splitting)
- **Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for dynamic aggregations and KPI indicators
